About this view

From understanding the risk
to agreeing what to protect.

This is the turning point in the conversation. It brings this family’s priorities, the financial consequences and the support already in place into one picture — so you and the client can agree what matters before discussing products.

01 · Their story This live fact-find

The home, the children, income and spending come from what this client has shared and the figures already on record for this case. Missing information stays visibly missing.

02 · Adviser bank Proven words, not a generic script

Picture and ask prompts are drawn live from Andrew’s own prompt bank, distilled from his real call recordings and 100 real practice cases. Claude selects a relevant line for this client and adapts it — it does not invent sales lines of its own. Each prompt below keeps its bank reference.

03 · Exact code Figures are never guessed by AI

Every figure on this page — the mortgage gap, the percentages, the years of income — is calculated by the app from what has been recorded for this case. Claude may phrase the explanation, but never invents or recalculates a number. Select “Behind this card” on any leaf to see its source.

This view reflects the real, live case open in the co-pilot right now. Discussion ticks stay in this browser tab until it is reloaded — they are not saved anywhere and are not client consent or a recommendation. “Save notes” downloads a local text file.
